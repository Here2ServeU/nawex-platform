# Express-Reliability AIOps: Predictive Clinical Safety Platform

## Executive Summary: Bridging the Gap Between AI and Infrastructure

The **Express-Reliability AIOps Platform** is an enterprise-grade ecosystem designed for high-availability healthcare environments. By integrating **Deep Learning (LSTM/TCN)** with a **Multi-Cloud Active-Active Resilience** strategy, the platform achieves 99.999% availability for critical [robotics telemetry streams](https://github.com/Here2ServeU/express-reliability-aiops-platform/tree/main/robotics), effectively transforming infrastructure from reactive to predictive.

## Architectural Excellence (Golden Project Standard)

This project utilizes the **T2S-Golden-Project** structure to ensure SOC 2 and [HIPAA-compliant](https://github.com/Here2ServeU/express-reliability-aiops-platform/new/main/docs/compliance) auditability and operational clarity.

* **[Core AI Brain](https://github.com/Here2ServeU/express-reliability-aiops-platform/tree/main/ai)**: Features a dual-model ensemble—utilizing LSTM for long-range trends and TCN for high-frequency anomalies—to deliver 92% or greater prediction precision.
* **[Multi-Cloud Topology](https://github.com/Here2ServeU/express-reliability-aiops-platform/blob/main/docs/architecture/03-infrastructure-topology.md)**: Provides global orchestration across **AWS, Azure, GCP, and OCI** through modular [Terraform](https://github.com/Here2ServeU/express-reliability-aiops-platform/tree/main/terraform) and [GitOps](https://github.com/Here2ServeU/express-reliability-aiops-platform/tree/main/gitops) frameworks.
* **[Resilience Engine](https://github.com/Here2ServeU/express-reliability-aiops-platform/blob/main/docs/architecture/05-active-active-resilience.md)**: Ensures a sub-30 second Recovery Time Objective (RTO) powered by [predictive failover logic](https://github.com/Here2ServeU/express-reliability-aiops-platform/blob/main/docs/architecture/04-predictive-reliability-framework.md).

## Ph.D. Research Implementation

The platform serves as a production-hardened implementation of advanced AI research focused on healthcare resilience:

* **Agentic Safety Mediation**: Conducts real-time kinematic telemetry analysis at the [Robotics Edge](https://github.com/Here2ServeU/express-reliability-aiops-platform/tree/main/robotics).
* **MLOps Lifecycle**: Enables continuous model evolution via [GCP Vertex AI](https://github.com/Here2ServeU/express-reliability-aiops-platform/blob/main/docs/architecture/03-infrastructure-topology.md) for compute-intensive re-training loops.
