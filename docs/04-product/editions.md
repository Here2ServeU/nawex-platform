# Editions

All editions share the same golden-path core: one package (`golden`), one image, the AI/decision
engine, the AI-safety controls (`golden.security`), the observability stack and GitOps delivery.
Editions differ by the business domain services they enable.

| Edition | Domain services | Primary buyer | Doc |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Healthcare** | ehr-ingestion, clinical-decision, patient-gateway | CIO / CMIO, clinical engineering | [healthcare-edition.md](./healthcare-edition.md) |
| **Fintech** | transaction-engine, fraud-detector, payment-gateway | CRO / Head of Payments | [fintech-edition.md](./fintech-edition.md) |
| **AI Infrastructure (GPU)** | gpu-optimizer | Head of AI Platform / FinOps | [ai-infra-edition.md](./ai-infra-edition.md) |
| **Modernization** | migration-planner (VMware → OpenShift Virtualization) | CIO / infrastructure leadership, SI partners | [../modernization/README.md](../modernization/README.md) |

Feature comparison: [feature-matrix.md](./feature-matrix.md).
