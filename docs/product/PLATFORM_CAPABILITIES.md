# Golden Platform Capabilities

## Product Mission

Golden Platform delivers AI-driven reliability for critical infrastructure in regulated industries. The product combines predictive intelligence, automated resilience, and multi-cloud controls to reduce outages, risk exposure, and operational drag.

## Capability Domains

### Reliability Intelligence

- Predictive anomaly detection using LSTM and TCN model families.
- Real-time risk scoring for transactions and clinical signals.
- Forecast-driven capacity and incident risk alerts.

### Automation and Remediation

- Policy-based auto-remediation workflows for common failure modes.
- Closed-loop incident handling from detection to stabilization.
- Operator override and approval hooks for high-risk actions.

### Event-Driven Core

- Durable event streams for telemetry and workflow orchestration.
- Kafka-backed asynchronous control-plane and data-plane workflows.
- Replay and audit support for forensic reliability analysis.

### Regulated Vertical Modules

- Fintech: transaction engine, fraud detection, payment resilience.
- Healthcare: EHR ingestion, clinical monitoring, compliance controls.
- Domain controls aligned to PCI-DSS, HIPAA, and SOX obligations.

### Multi-Cloud and Platform Engineering

- Infrastructure modules for Terraform, Kubernetes, Helm, and networking.
- GitOps promotion and environment orchestration.
- Workload portability across AWS, Azure, GCP, and OCI patterns.

## Enterprise Product KPIs

- Reliability: availability and SLO attainment by domain.
- Safety: incident MTTD and MTTR reductions.
- Financial: loss prevented, downtime cost avoided, fraud avoided.
- Compliance: control coverage and audit pass rates.
- Delivery: release frequency with policy and quality gates passing.

## Roadmap Themes

- Reliability Copilot: explainable remediation recommendations.
- Autonomous failover orchestration across cloud boundaries.
- Adaptive anomaly models with per-tenant drift controls.
- Executive reliability scorecards mapped to business outcomes.
