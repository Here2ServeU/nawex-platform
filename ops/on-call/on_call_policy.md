# On-Call Policy

## Objectives
- Ensure 24/7 coverage for critical services.
- Minimize mean-time-to-detect (MTTD) and mean-time-to-resolve (MTTR).
- Provide a humane, sustainable rotation for engineers.

## Coverage Model
- Primary on-call: 1 engineer
- Secondary on-call: 1 engineer
- Shadow on-call (optional, trainees)

## Expectations
- Acknowledge P1 pages within 5 minutes.
- Acknowledge P2 pages within 15 minutes.
- Use runbooks in `/ops/incidents` and `/ops/playbooks`.
- All changes for mitigation go through GitOps where possible.

## Handover
- Daily handoff notes in Slack / Teams.
- Review open incidents, ongoing investigations, and risk areas.
