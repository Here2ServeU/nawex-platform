# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability
Please report security issues privately to the repository owner and avoid public disclosure until remediation is available.

## Scope
This applies to all code, infrastructure manifests, CI/CD, and security policy artifacts in this repository.

## Response targets
- Acknowledgement: 2 business days
- Initial triage: 5 business days
- Mitigation plan: 10 business days (severity-dependent)
