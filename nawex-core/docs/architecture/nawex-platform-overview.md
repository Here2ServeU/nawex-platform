# NAWEX Platform Overview

NAWEX is organized to support five purposes at once:

1. SaaS platform
2. PhD research platform
3. GitHub engineering portfolio
4. Training ecosystem
5. Investor-ready architecture

## Core Layers

- AI prediction and explainability
- Decision engine and policy routing
- Automation and self-healing workflows
- Observability and incident intelligence
- Domain verticals for fintech and healthcare
- Compliance and audit readiness
