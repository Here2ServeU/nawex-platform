### Architectural Domains: My Role in Solving Problems at Scale

#### 1. Predictive AIOps Engine (`ai/`) - The Brain

* **Problem:** Traditional monitoring is *reactive*, alerting *after* a failure impacts customers.
* **Solution:** Custom **LSTM** and **TCN** machine learning models trained on telemetry to forecast resource exhaustion and latency spikes *before* they manifest.
* **Result:** Predicts critical incidents up to 30 minutes in advance, enabling auto-remediation to *prevent 85% of potential outages*.

#### 2. Agentic Clinical Safety (`robotics/`) - The Future

* **Problem:** Cloud-connected medical robots face "Operational Fragility"—milliseconds of jitter can desync surgical precision.
* **Solution:** A specialized **Clinical Fragility Simulator** that monitors Kinematic Telemetry (motor vibration, torque) alongside network RTT.
* **Result:** Autonomous agents intercept failing packets and reroute to **Edge Compute** nodes before mechanical failure occurs.

#### 3. Infrastructure as Code (`terraform/`) - The Foundation

* **Problem:** Manual provisioning leads to "Snowflake Servers," which are inconsistent and prone to error.
* **Solution:** Modular, cloud-agnostic Terraform code for **EKS, AKS, GKE, and OKE**. Includes automated provisioning of high-performance **Redis** clusters for low-latency AI task brokering.
* **Result:** Reduces environment provisioning time *from days to minutes*.

#### 4. Continuous Governance (`gitops/`) - The Guardrails

* **Problem:** "Configuration Drift" causes impossible-to-debug outages and fails compliance audits.
* **Solution:** Utilizing **ArgoCD and Flux** to enforce a "Code-is-Law" policy.
* **Result:** Achieves **zero configuration drift** and provides an immutable audit trail for **HIPAA/SOC 2 compliance**.

#### 5. Site Reliability Engineering (`sre/`) - The Metric

* **Problem:** "Reliability" is often vague, leading to misalignment between Product and Engineering.
* **Solution:** Defined mathematical **Service Level Objectives (SLOs)** and **Error Budgets** with formalized Chaos Engineering.
* **Result:** Reduces **Mean Time To Recovery (MTTR) by 50%**.
